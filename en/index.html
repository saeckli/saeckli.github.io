Säckli

The cornhole scorer for iPhone. The phone watches the board and counts every round: bags in the hole and on the board.

Set up

Put the iPhone on a tripod, 2 to 3 metres from the board, at about head height.

Play

Start the round, throw, end the round. The app proposes the result.

Confirm

You check it and fix it if needed. At the end there is a highlight video.

Modes

  • Game: singles or doubles to 21, with the score and who starts next.
  • Säckli Ten: ten rounds on your own, with points and stats.
  • Free round and Two players: training without stats.

Private

Säckli needs no account and no internet connection. The recordings stay on your iPhone. More under Privacy.

Beta

Säckli is still in testing and only available through TestFlight, not in the App Store. If you would like to test it, write to feedback@saeckli.ch. How testing works.
