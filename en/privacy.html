Privacy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short: Säckli has no account, no ads and no analytics. Camera and sound are processed on your phone and stay there. The app connects to the internet only when you send a round yourself or play a tournament with online sync; the tournament data is then encrypted so that we cannot read it.

Who is responsible

Marco Meyer, Switzerland. Contact: feedback@saeckli.ch

What the app processes on your phone

Säckli is available for iPhone and Android. Since the test version of 27 November 2026 the Android app also counts with the camera (games and training); since the test version of 27 November 2026 it sends rounds as on the iPhone. The app also reads QR codes with the camera.

  • Camera: to recognise the bags on the board. Recognition runs on the phone.
  • Microphone (optional): to hear bags landing on the board. On the iPhone the sounds are also classified, for example to tell speech from an impact; on Android only the loudness counts. All of it on the phone.
  • Recordings: each round is stored on the phone as a video with sound, together with the recognition data, the result and your corrections. Videos are deleted after 7 days, except rounds you want to send; you can change this in the settings.
  • Game data: team names, scores, training stats and settings.
  • Player profiles and tournaments: the players' names, their results and stats, the tournaments with their pairings and standings. You pass a profile on with a QR code.
  • Bluetooth: connected games and tournaments exchange scores directly between the phones nearby, without a server.
  • Photos: the app only asks to add a highlight video to your photos, and only when you choose to save it. It does not read your photos.

This data stays in the app on your phone (and, for connected games and tournaments, on the other players' phones). When you delete the app, it is gone. It may be included in phone backups, as with other apps.

When you send a round

Only when you start it yourself (Settings › Recordings) does the app pack the chosen rounds, one file each. Each file contains:

  • the videos of those rounds, with sound,
  • the recognition data, the result and your corrections,
  • your note and the team names,
  • the app version, the phone model, the iOS or Android version and a random ID of this installation (so one phone's rounds stay together; it is not linked to you, your Apple ID or your Google account).

Uploading: the files go encrypted (HTTPS) to storage at Cloudflare (Cloudflare R2, located in the European Union). Cloudflare technically sees your IP address; we do not store it. Uploaded rounds are deleted from that storage after 180 days at the latest; what we need for the recognition is then kept only by us.

Sharing as a file: instead, you can send the rounds through the iOS share sheet (email, AirDrop, WhatsApp, ...); that service's own terms apply to it.

We use sent rounds only to improve bag recognition, including training the recognition models. We do not pass them on or publish them. We delete them on request.

The videos show the players and have sound. Ask the people in the picture before you send.

Online sync of tournaments

A tournament can sync its data through a server, so results reach everyone without Bluetooth. Whoever sets up the tournament chooses this (New tournament › Online sync). For a club championship it is preset, because not everyone meets there; it can be turned off when setting it up.

  • What: the tournament's entries (players, pairings, results). They are encrypted end to end on the phone. The key is in the tournament's QR code and invite link and never reaches the server: we cannot read the contents, not even names.
  • Where: in a database at Cloudflare (Cloudflare D1, located in the European Union), at go.saeckli.com. The server sees only random IDs and the number, size and time of the entries. Cloudflare technically sees your IP address; it is used only to limit too many requests, and we do not store it.
  • How long: a tournament is deleted when nobody has accessed it for 400 days; earlier on request.
  • Invite links (go.saeckli.com/…): what the tournament needs is after the "#" in the link. A browser does not send that part to the server; the link opens the app.

Beta through TestFlight and as an Android file

During the beta, Säckli is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. Apple shares with us your name and email address if we invited you by email, crash reports, feedback you send in TestFlight, and technical details such as the iPhone model and iOS version. Apple's TestFlight privacy notice applies to this.

You download the Android beta as a file from a link at Cloudflare (upload.saeckli.com). Cloudflare technically sees your IP address; we do not store it and receive nothing else.

What there is not

  • no account and no login,
  • no ads and no tracking,
  • no analytics or statistics services,
  • no connection to a server, except to upload rounds you send and for the online sync of tournaments that have it turned on.

Possible optional features later

Later there may be, for example, anonymous usage statistics or other online features. Such features would be optional. We will update this policy before such a feature comes to the app.

Your rights

You can ask what data you sent us and have it deleted. Write to feedback@saeckli.ch.
